# BOUNCAMPUS — 90-Second KREATE Jury Demo

## Demo objective

Do **not** try to show the whole platform. In 90 seconds the jury should understand exactly five things:

1. the problem is real and already measured;
2. the product makes a next-service operating decision;
3. model uncertainty changes whether the product recommends action;
4. a human operator remains in control;
5. climate impact is only claimed after a falsifiable pilot.

## One-line product

> **BOUNCAMPUS turns measured institutional food waste into an uncertainty-aware, human-approved production decision and a controlled pilot that can prove the product wrong.**

## Before walking on stage

Open these tabs in order:

1. `/demo`
2. official Boğaziçi food-waste source
3. `/food-waste`
4. `/api/v1/food`

Do not start on GitHub, architecture diagrams, 3D map, energy, mobility or the data page.

If live contextual sources are degraded, that is not a demo failure. Use it to demonstrate `REVIEW_REQUIRED` / `WITHHOLD` and say the product degrades safely instead of inventing certainty.

---

## 0–18s — PROBLEM

### On screen

Stay at the top of `/demo` and point to:

- **48,251 kg** 2025 food waste;
- **33,430 kg** recovery;
- official-source provenance.

### Say

> “Boğaziçi already measures the problem: 48,251 kilograms of food waste in 2025. The first number you see is not generated by our model. We start from the institution’s own baseline.”

### If challenged

Open the official source immediately. Do not debate the number from memory.

### Do not say

- “we already reduced 48 tons”;
- “48 tons is all avoidable”;
- any unsupported carbon/water conversion.

---

## 18–40s — DECISION + UNCERTAINTY

### On screen

Scroll to the demand band.

Point to:

- predicted meals;
- lower / starting / upper values;
- signal coverage;
- readiness badge;
- source pills.

### Say

> “The missing step is not another waste report. Before the next service, BOUNCAMPUS combines the course schedule with weather, menu and academic-calendar context. But it never hides uncertainty. We return a band plus a readiness state.”

Then:

> “If contextual evidence gets weaker, the band widens. If critical evidence is insufficient, the product says WITHHOLD instead of producing a confident-looking number.”

Point to `MODEL_ESTIMATE`.

### Do not say

- “this is real cafeteria demand”;
- “we are connected to POS”;
- “this is production telemetry.”

## 40–55s — HUMAN GATE

### On screen

Press **Approve controlled pilot** if the state is `PILOT_READY`.

If it is not `PILOT_READY`, press **Hold / review** instead. That is an equally valid demo.

Point to:

```text
AUTO_DISPATCH=false
```

### Say

> “AI does not send a kitchen command. A human operator approves, edits or holds every recommendation. Even this demo does not fake an external kitchen integration.”

### Why this matters

This is where BOUNCAMPUS becomes an operations product instead of a prediction page.

---

## 55–70s — SCENARIO

### On screen

Move the prevention slider once.

Do not spend time tuning a perfect number.

### Say

> “This applies a target to the official historical baseline so the operator can understand scale. It is scenario math, not achieved savings.”

Point to `SCENARIO_NOT_RESULT`.

### Judge trap to avoid

If asked “so you save this many kilograms?”, answer:

> “No. That is the modeled target scale. The next section defines the measurement required before we can claim any savings.”

---

## 70–90s — EVIDENCE / FAILURE GATE

### On screen

Show:

- `kg / 100 served meals`;
- `≥10%` pilot target;
- `5 + 5` minimum measured services;
- download CSV button.

### Say

> “We define failure before running the pilot. Our primary KPI is waste kilograms per 100 served meals, so a quieter day cannot look artificially better. We target at least 10% reduction versus matched control, with no increase in early sell-out and no food-safety shortcut.”

Then close:

> **“We do not let the AI declare victory. The measured pilot does.”**

# 3-minute expanded version

If the jury gives more time, add these points after the 90-second flow.

## Why normalize by served meals?

> “Raw kilograms per service are confounded by service volume. We normalize by served meals so we compare operating efficiency rather than simply comparing a quiet day with a busy day.”

## Why the model should be able to WITHHOLD

> “A production system that always gives an answer is unsafe. Source health is part of the product. Missing context changes the decision, not just a small badge in the corner.”

## Why no CO2 number on the main screen?

> “First we measure the direct physical outcome—food waste mass. Carbon and water equivalents come later from a documented lifecycle factor. We do not multiply a model estimate by another assumption and call the result measured impact.”

## Why this can scale

> “The campus-specific signals can change, but the operating contract does not: provenance, uncertainty, human approval, matched measurement and recalibration. That transfers to hospitals, factories, schools and catering operators.”

# Judge questions — fast answers

## “Is the 48,251 kg number real?”

Yes. It is Boğaziçi University’s published 2025 food-waste total. The product links directly to the source.

## “Are the recommended meal numbers real production data?”

No. They are explicitly `MODEL_ESTIMATE`. The first pilot calibrates them against measured produced, served, surplus and waste data.

## “Are you connected to cafeteria POS?”

No. We do not claim POS access. The first pilot can run with aggregate service-level measurements and no student/payment identity.

## “Why AI/modeling at all?”

Because demand changes with academic schedules, calendar effects, weather and menu context. The pilot tests whether that context beats the existing planning process. If it does not, the hypothesis fails.

## “What exactly is the pilot?”

Fourteen days of matched control/intervention services. Each service records forecast, produced, served, surplus, waste, early-sellout and operator override. Primary KPI: waste kg per 100 served meals.

## “Why not just reduce production aggressively?”

Because early sell-out is a guardrail. Waste reduction that degrades meal availability is not counted as success.

## “Why is this climate tech?”

It acts before avoidable surplus is produced. The direct physical outcome is less food wasted; broader climate equivalents are added only after measured waste reduction and documented factors.

## “What happened to energy, mobility and 3D?”

They remain implemented expansion modules. KREATE leads with one measurable climate outcome so the jury can understand the product and its evidence end to end.

## “What would prove you wrong?”

Less than the pre-registered normalized reduction, increased early sell-out, poor measurement quality, or an unusably high override pattern. We designed the pilot to reveal those outcomes rather than hide them.

# Presenter discipline

- Say **official**, **model**, **scenario**, and **measured** precisely.
- Never use “live” for data that is not live.
- Never say “saved” for a scenario.
- Never imply the approve button dispatches to a real kitchen.
- Do not open secondary modules unless the core story has already landed.
- If a source is degraded, demonstrate safe degradation rather than apologizing for it.
- If asked a number you cannot support, say which measurement would be required to support it.
