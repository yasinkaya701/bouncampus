**Purpose:** align interview sequencing with the Google Form requirement that teams conduct **at least 3 customer interviews with people who fit the Persona**, while still preserving supporting data/governance interviews.  
**Status:** interview-planning artifact. **No interview/outreach is implied.**

## Executive conclusion

The first three interview entries that appear in the KREATE form should ideally be **customer/persona-fit operational actors**, not an IT data owner or generic sustainability stakeholder.

Recommended first-three evidence structure:

```text
1. Boğaziçi Food Services operational/governance route
2. Current TEMAŞ Boğaziçi production/project owner
3. Same operational Persona at a second institution (GTÜ or İTÜ preferred)
```

Then conduct supporting interviews with:

```text
BUCard/IT data owner
waste-measurement / Zero Waste owner
procurement/economic buyer
additional second/third institutions
```

The supporting interviews remain extremely valuable, but they should not be the only interviews used to satisfy the form's customer/persona requirement.

# Form Key Insights structure

Each of the first three form interviews should be summarized around behavior, not opinion:

```text
Profile
  actual role + relation to decision

Key Insights
  last concrete incident
  current decision workflow
  actual alternative/tool
  consequence / tradeoff
  surprise
  hypothesis supported/rejected/modified
```

Avoid:

> They liked the idea.

Prefer:

> The interview changed H-A because daily quantity is actually locked at X and only campus allocation remains adjustable after Y.

---

# Selection rule for the final three displayed in the form

If more than three interviews are completed, choose the three that best demonstrate:

1. **Persona fit**;
2. **distinct evidence** rather than repeated confirmation;
3. **a real changed assumption**;
4. **one or more falsifying/negative insights**;
5. **cross-site repeatability or segmentation learning**.

Do not simply choose the three most enthusiastic interviewees.

```text
Boğaziçi Food Services
        ↓ referral

        ↓

```

Then supporting data/waste/buyer interviews.

```text
we talked to the institutional owner

```

rather than three internal stakeholders around one unvalidated assumption.
