## Questions that must be answered from contract/source owner

1. What is the contractual work item and unit used for payment?
2. Which quantity becomes payable/accepted: requested, produced, delivered, served, accepted, reconciled, or another quantity?
3. Who signs/approves acceptance and hakediş?
4. At what grain is quantity committed: day, meal period, campus, menu item, category, or another unit?
5. When is total production frozen? When is campus allocation frozen? Can batch/replenishment quantities still change later?
6. Who can authorize a quantity change and through which document/system?
7. How are additional meals, second meals, package meals, cancellations and corrections handled?
8. What is the authoritative record when BUCard/passages disagree with production or acceptance records?
9. Who bears the economic consequence of overproduction or unused prepared food?
10. What is the operational/contractual consequence of shortage, early sellout, late service or quality failure?
11. Is there a minimum/committed quantity or capacity obligation that changes the optimization boundary?
12. Could a human-reviewed decision-support recommendation legally and operationally affect quantity before the relevant freeze?
