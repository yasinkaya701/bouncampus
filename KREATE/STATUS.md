## Current PMR hypotheses

### H-A — controllable decision

A named operator determines/approves production or allocation quantity before demand is known and has a non-zero adjustment window before a freeze point.

### H-B — material mismatch

Demand/planning mismatch repeatedly creates addressable edible surplus and/or shortages that can be affected by changing the reachable decision.

### H-C — asymmetric risk

Shortage is sufficiently costly/visible that operators use an explicit or implicit safety buffer based on historical experience/fragmented information.

These are **not supported yet** merely because analogous mechanisms exist elsewhere or because TEMAŞ publicly discusses demand-aligned planning.

## Current kill/modify gates

Modify or kill the pre-service production wedge if repeated PMR shows:

- quantity is not a reachable decision;
- the useful freeze point does not exist;
- addressable edible surplus is not material;
- waste is mainly caused by taste/quality/portion/menu/prep or another controllable stage;
- reservation/current systems already solve the core problem sufficiently;
- shortage/safety constraints make quantity reduction unacceptable;
- outcomes cannot be measured at acceptable effort;
- an incumbent system already solves the decision adequately;
- no reachable sponsor captures enough operational/economic value;
- the same product/sales process does not repeat across the proposed beachhead.
