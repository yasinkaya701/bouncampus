# KREATE Role System Summary

This file is a short navigation layer for the five execution-role documents. The execution-role count does not imply the human-team count; the KREATE team remains four humans.

- `README.md` — autonomous operating principles; roles are ownership, not confinement.
- `01_IE_CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY_MARKET_LEAD.md` — market reality, customer discovery, beachhead and operational evidence.
- `02_EE_PHYSICAL_SYSTEMS_MEASUREMENT_LEAD.md` — physical measurement, instrumentation, calibration validity and pilot evidence.
- `03_CS1_DECISION_INTELLIGENCE_LEAD.md` — decision formulation, baselines, models, uncertainty and evaluation.
- `04_CS2_PRODUCT_STRATEGY_EVIDENCE_APPLICATION_LEAD.md` — product direction, synthesis, differentiation and application narrative.
- `05_EHB_EMBEDDED_HARDWARE_COMMUNICATIONS_INTEGRATION_LEAD.md` — embedded electronics, PCB, firmware, communications, device-side interfaces, bring-up and HW↔SW integration.

## Shared Rules

1. Any role may cross into another workstream when strategically useful.
2. Any role may open a bounded experiment without waiting for routine approval.
3. PMR is distributed across all four human members; autonomous role count is separate from human count.
4. Evidence may change the product, beachhead, feature set, hardware plan or model strategy.
5. Role documents are guides for judgment and ownership, not fixed checklists.
6. Important claims must remain distinguishable as fact, measurement, estimate, interpretation or hypothesis.
7. AI is encouraged for speed and rigor, but never as a substitute for real evidence.
8. EE owns measurement/calibration truth; EHB owns embedded implementation; CS1 owns downstream model/decision semantics. Cross-boundary schema changes require affected-role review.
