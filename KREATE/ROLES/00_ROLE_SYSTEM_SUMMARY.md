## Shared Rules

1. Any role may cross into another workstream when strategically useful.
2. Any role may open a bounded experiment without waiting for routine approval.
3. PMR is distributed across all four members.
4. Evidence may change the product, beachhead, feature set, hardware plan or model strategy.
5. Role documents are guides for judgment and ownership, not fixed checklists.
6. Important claims must remain distinguishable as fact, measurement, estimate, interpretation or hypothesis.
7. AI is encouraged for speed and rigor, but never as a substitute for real evidence.

## Current Application-Stage Goal

Maximize the probability of reaching the KREATE Top 15 by October 8 through stronger evidence, sharper product decisions, credible technology and a coherent application.
