# Türkiye Policy, Ranking & Institutional Pull — Agent Brief

**Research date:** 2026-10-01  
**Status:** Secondary/public-source research. Not PMR and not customer validation.

## Why this file exists

BOUNCAMPUS needs a defensible answer to **why now?** and **why universities?** without pretending policy/ranking pressure proves willingness to buy this product. This brief isolates external forces that make measurement, digital governance, operational efficiency and verifiable sustainability performance increasingly relevant to Turkish universities.

---

## T-01 — YÖK is explicitly pushing sustainable and climate-friendly campus transformation

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

YÖK's Sustainable and Climate-Friendly Campus initiative covers energy efficiency, renewable energy, zero waste, sustainable/climate-friendly buildings and related financing/support mechanisms. YÖK initially named 11 pilot universities and has stated an intent to broaden the approach beyond the pilots.

Official/primary sources:

- https://www.yok.gov.tr/Sayfalar/Kurumsal/IdariBirimler/proje-gelistirme-destekleme-dairesi/diger-onemli-is-birlikleri-projeler-programlar.aspx
- https://basinda.metu.edu.tr/icerik/odtuden/250

**Research inference:** Turkish universities face increasing institutional pressure to demonstrate operational sustainability, but this does not establish a specific software budget or buyer.

---

## T-02 — National university data already shows material sustainability investment

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

YÖK reported in March 2025 that:

- 62 universities generated about **77 GWh** of electricity from renewable sources in one year;
- 33 universities supplied water from transformed/treated sources, with that water equal to about **16%** of total consumption for those institutions;
- 45 universities invested roughly **25 million TL** in water-saving measures.

YÖK's English release explicitly lists **Boğaziçi University among the universities with the highest water-saving investment values**.

Primary source:

https://www.yok.gov.tr/en/Sayfalar/news/2025/turkiye-builds-sustainable-campuses-with-green-universities-and-zero-waste.aspx

**Product implication:** a value proposition centered on "start measuring sustainability" is too weak for mature institutions. A more credible wedge is to improve how existing measurements influence decisions and how results are verified.

## T-03 — Covered public buildings have a 2030 energy-saving target

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

The Ministry of Energy and Natural Resources states that public buildings subject to the energy-manager obligation are targeted to achieve at least **30% energy savings by 2030** under the relevant public-building savings program.

Primary source:

https://www.enerji.gov.tr/evced-enerji-verimliligi-kamu-binalarinda-tasarruf-hedefi

**Research inference:** facilities/energy teams have stronger incentives to identify, prioritize and document savings. This strengthens future energy-module relevance; it does not prove Boğaziçi would purchase BOUNCAMPUS.

## T-04 — Türkiye's climate-policy environment is becoming more formal

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

Türkiye's first Climate Law was adopted in 2025, creating a national framework for mitigation, adaptation, planning and institutional implementation.

Primary source:

https://iklim.gov.tr/turkiyenin-ilk-iklim-kanunu-tbmm-genel-kurulu-nda-kabul-edilerek-yasalasti-haber-4508

**Research inference:** sustainability reporting, planning and evidence quality are becoming less optional at institutional level over time. Do not turn this into a legal-compliance claim for BOUNCAMPUS unless legal applicability is reviewed separately.

## T-05 — UI GreenMetric changed in 2026 in a direction highly relevant to BOUNCAMPUS

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

UI GreenMetric Sustainable University Rankings 2026 added a seventh pillar: **Governance & Digitalization (GD)**. The official 2026 guideline specifically asks universities about:

- **GD6:** ICT use for sustainability-program planning, implementation, monitoring and evaluation;
- **GD7:** policies for advanced digital technologies such as AI and IoT to support decision-making, operational efficiency and service delivery.

The 2026 theme is **"Advancing Sustainable Campuses through Governance, Digitalization, and Integrated Performance."**

Primary sources:

- https://uigreenmetric.com/rankings/university/overall-rankings-2026
- https://uigreenmetric.com/rankings/university
- https://uigreenmetric.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/2026_Guideline_UI-GreenMetric-SUR-eng-v2.pdf

**Important boundary:** ranking criteria are market/context evidence. They do not prove a university needs our exact product.

**Product implication:** the proposition "decision + verification + evidence/provenance" maps better to current institutional direction than a simple sustainability-awareness application.

## T-06 — Türkiye has a large, active sustainable-campus benchmark set

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

The official 2026 UI GreenMetric ranking lists **2,016 institutions in 110 countries**. Selected Turkish institutions include:

- İstanbul Technical University — **world rank 28**;
- Yıldız Technical University — **45**;
- Erciyes University — **63**;
- Ege University — **74**;
- Özyeğin University — **86**;
- Middle East Technical University — **105**.

Primary source:

https://uigreenmetric.com/rankings/university/overall-rankings-2026

ITU also states that it ranked first in Türkiye for the tenth consecutive year in 2026:

https://haberler.itu.edu.tr/haberdetay/2026/09/30/itu-10-yildir-turkiye-nin-en-surdurulebilir-universitesi

**Research inference:** a Turkey-wide product cannot assume customers are sustainability beginners. The benchmark includes institutions with mature sustainability offices, monitoring, reporting and campus programs.

## T-07 — ODTÜ independently identifies data governance, measurement and transparency as a strategic sustainability theme

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

ODTÜ's 2025 Climate Action Plan co-design process was organized around five themes, one of which was **Data Governance, Measurement, and Transparency**. Other themes included energy/transport/built environment and circular economy/water/waste.

Primary source:

https://iklim.metu.edu.tr/en/announcements/metu-climate-action-plan-co-design-workshop-took-place

ODTÜ's sustainable-campus portal also publishes operational sustainability indicators and annual reports:

https://sustainablecampus.metu.edu.tr/

**Product implication:** provenance and verification are not cosmetic features. They align with an emerging institutional governance concern that is visible outside Boğaziçi as well.

# Why-now narrative that is safe to use

A defensible secondary-research narrative is:

> Turkish universities are investing in energy, water and zero-waste programs while national policy and international sustainability frameworks increasingly reward measurable performance, governance and digitalization. This creates a credible environment for tools that help institutions turn operational data into traceable decisions and verified outcomes.

This statement is **market context**, not customer validation.

# PMR implications

Ask institutional stakeholders whether these external forces actually change their work:

1. Which external sustainability reports/rankings/regulations consume staff time today?
2. Which metrics require manual reconciliation across units?
3. Does a ranking or YÖK request ever change an operational decision, or only reporting work?
4. Who owns evidence collection for energy, water, waste and transport?
5. Which sustainability intervention was most difficult to verify after implementation?
6. Would better measurement influence budget allocation, or is budget decided independently?
7. Are ICT/AI/IoT sustainability capabilities a strategic priority or merely reporting language?
8. What evidence would be required before a recommendation can influence a facilities/dining decision?

# Claim firewall

Do not claim:

- policy creates a legal requirement to buy BOUNCAMPUS;
- GreenMetric participation equals willingness to pay;
- all Turkish universities have the same maturity;
- Boğaziçi participates in the 2026 UI GreenMetric ranking unless separately verified;
- ranking points are guaranteed by implementing BOUNCAMPUS;
- a national total is Boğaziçi-specific performance.
