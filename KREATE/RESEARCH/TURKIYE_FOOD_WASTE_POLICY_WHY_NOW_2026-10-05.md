# Türkiye Food-Waste Policy / Why-Now Context

**Research date:** 2026-10-05
**Status:** Official-policy secondary research. This supports policy salience, **not customer willingness-to-pay** or BOUNCAMPUS impact.

## 1. Food loss/waste reduction is an explicit current national policy objective

Türkiye Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry 2024–2028 Strategic Plan:
https://www.tarim.gov.tr/SGB/Belgeler/stratejikplan.pdf

Under its food-safety objective, the plan explicitly includes:
- improving food-safety practices;
- reducing food loss and waste;
- projects to reduce / recover agriculture-food industry waste;
- awareness activity targets;
- a stated strategy to pursue legal regulation for prevention of food loss and waste.

### Implication

Food-waste prevention is not only an ESG marketing topic; it is present in current national strategic planning.

### Boundary

This does not prove that universities have dedicated software budget for the problem.

## 2. Active inter-agency Food Waste Working Group in 2026

Official Ministry update, 2 October 2026:
https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/Haber/7304/Gida-Israfi-Calisma-Grubu-12-Toplantisini-Gerceklestirdi

The Food Waste Working Group held its **12th meeting** with participation from multiple national bodies including:
- Presidency Agriculture and Food Policies Board;
- Ministry of Environment, Urbanization and Climate Change;
- Treasury and Finance;
- Trade;
- TurkStat;
- Central Bank;
- Culture and Tourism;
- Family and Social Services;
- Ministry of National Education.

The meeting addressed:
- reducing food waste;
- efficient resource use;
- future action and cross-institution coordination.

An earlier 10th meeting took place in May 2026:
https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/GKGM/Haber/1307/

### Implication

The issue remains active at policy level in 2026 rather than being only a legacy campaign.

## 3. National strategy / guidance exists for food loss and waste

Ministry publication hub:
https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/ABDGM/Sayfalar/Detay.aspx?SayfaId=3

The Ministry publicly provides:
- National Strategy Document and Action Plan on prevention, reduction and management of food loss/waste;
- sustainable food-systems roadmap;
- guide specifically for **hotels, restaurants and other mass-consumption food-service locations**;
- logistics-sector food-loss guide;
- composting guide.

### Product implication

The food-service sector already has prevention guidance. BOUNCAMPUS must therefore be a measurable operational implementation mechanism, not an awareness brochure or generic food-waste education product.

## 4. Prevention before downstream treatment

Türkiye's national food-loss/waste strategy has historically been framed around a hierarchy where prevention comes before rescue/redistribution and downstream recovery.

Current global-methodology alignment is also consistent with EPA/UNEP prevention-first logic recorded elsewhere in the repository.

### Safe climate framing

> BOUNCAMPUS tests whether avoidable surplus can be prevented at a production decision before the food becomes waste.

### Unsafe climate framing

> BOUNCAMPUS is proven to reduce Türkiye's food waste.

No such evidence exists.

## 5. Why this matters specifically for institutional kitchens

Institutional dining is a useful test setting because:
- production is repeated at scale;
- demand is variable but observable after service;
- operations may already collect electronic entry / consumption records;
- service continuity prevents blindly minimizing production;
- interventions can be prospectively measured.

This conclusion comes from the combined university/procurement/academic research, not from policy documents alone.

## 6. Do not overstate policy pull

Policy salience does **not** equal buyer demand.

PMR must still test:
- which policy/ranking/reporting requirements create real workload;
- whether food-waste reduction affects budget/procurement decisions;
- who owns the metric;
- whether operational improvement receives funding;
- whether universities/contractors are willing to pay for decision software.

## 7. Application use

Good use:
> `Food-waste prevention remains an active national priority in Türkiye, while universities operate repeated high-volume food-service decisions. We are testing a measurable prevention intervention at one operational control point.`

Bad use:
> `New regulations require universities to buy our solution.`

No reviewed source supports that claim.

## 8. Bottom line

The why-now case is stronger as a combination:

```text
national food-waste prevention priority
+ university sustainability/digital-governance pressure
+ high-volume repeated dining decisions
+ available digital/operational signals
+ maturing measurement/AI technology
```

But customer urgency must still be established through PMR, not inferred from policy.
