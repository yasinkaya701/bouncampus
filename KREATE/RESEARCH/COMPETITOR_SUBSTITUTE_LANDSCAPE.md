# Competitor & Substitute Landscape — Türkiye-first, Boğaziçi-centered

**Research date:** 2026-10-01  
**Status:** Public-source competitor research. Product/vendor claims are based on public materials and should be rechecked before final application use.

## Executive conclusion

The most important competitive finding is that **"campus sustainability data + evidence + GreenMetric/THE reporting" is already a product category in Türkiye**. BOUNCAMPUS cannot claim that centralized sustainability evidence/provenance by itself is unique.

The strongest differentiation to test is narrower and more operational:

> **BOUNCAMPUS should sit between campus data and a specific resource decision, then verify whether the human action changed a measured outcome.**

For the first wedge, that means dining-production decisions rather than generic sustainability reporting.

# C-01 — Direct Türkiye competitor: Emissary Campus

`PUBLIC SOURCE — vendor claims / live public deployment`

Emissary Campus markets itself specifically to higher education as a sustainability management and carbon-footprint platform aligned with **UI GreenMetric and THE Impact**. Its public materials describe:

- role-based campus sustainability data management;
- evidence/document management;
- carbon, water, waste, energy and transportation tracking;
- GreenMetric/THE reporting support;
- audit/approval workflows and traceability;
- decision-support positioning around sustainability data.

Vendor page:
https://emissary.com.tr/tr/cozumler/emissary-campus

A public Emissary Campus instance exists under the **İzmir Institute of Technology (İYTE)** domain and exposes a visitor dashboard with carbon and water indicators, indicating at least one visible institutional deployment/integration context:

https://emissarycampus.iyte.edu.tr/

Emissary's 2026 GreenMetric article explicitly frames the problem as how sustainability data are generated, validated, governed and transformed into institutional decisions:

https://emissary.com.tr/tr/akademi/hizli-icgoru/2026-ui-greenmetric-surdurulebilir-universite-siralamasi-kanita-dayali-kampus-yonetimi-yonetisim-ve-dijital-surdurulebilirlik

## Implication for BOUNCAMPUS

**Provenance, reporting and sustainability dashboards are not sufficient differentiation.**

If BOUNCAMPUS remains broad, Emissary appears to overlap strongly with:

- evidence collection;
- carbon/resource tracking;
- ranking/reporting support;
- cross-unit data governance.

Candidate differentiation that must be validated:

1. **operational decision objects**, not only sustainability indicators;
2. short-cycle forecasting and optimization before a resource is consumed/wasted;
3. explicit `recommendation -> operator action -> measured outcome` loop;
4. service-level guardrails and human override;
5. domain-specific dining production logic before cross-domain expansion.

Do not say Emissary lacks any of these capabilities unless independently verified. Ask buyers to compare actual workflows.

# C-02 — Food-waste specialist: Winnow

`PUBLIC SOURCE — vendor claims`

Winnow markets AI-powered food-waste prevention to university dining. Its university page describes:

- kitchen waste measurement by weight/time/category;
- image/camera-assisted recognition products;
- plate-waste measurement;
- production guidance around term-time demand swings;
- auditable food-waste reporting;
- multi-site benchmarking.

Source:
https://www.winnowsolutions.com/industries/universities

Winnow says it operates across thousands of kitchens internationally. Public vendor results/cost-savings percentages are **vendor claims**, not neutral benchmarks and not expected Boğaziçi effects.

## Implication

If BOUNCAMPUS becomes only "AI that tells the kitchen what food is wasted," it enters a mature specialist category.

Potential differentiation to test:

- combine campus-specific demand context (academic calendar/service regime/menu/authorized occupancy signals) with production decisions;
- work initially with existing institutional data rather than requiring proprietary waste-vision hardware;
- integrate decision evidence into a broader campus resource-governance layer.

But this differentiation is a hypothesis until buyers compare alternatives.

# C-03 — Food-waste specialist: Leanpath

`PUBLIC SOURCE — vendor claims`

Leanpath has a dedicated college/university offering centered on food-waste measurement, root-cause visibility, operational action and sustainability reporting.

Source:
https://www.leanpath.com/industries/college-university/

Its public university case studies describe teams using waste data to identify issues in preparation, forecasting and recipes. Again, case-study percentages are external/vendor claims and should not be used as BOUNCAMPUS impact estimates.

## Implication

The category already understands **measurement -> root cause -> kitchen action**. BOUNCAMPUS must be more specific than "actionable food-waste dashboard."

The unresolved question is whether Boğaziçi needs:

- better waste measurement,
- better demand forecasting before production,
- better workflow/contract coordination,
- or none of the above.

PMR must separate these.

# C-04 — Existing BMS/BEMS are the energy-module incumbent

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

Turkish universities already deploy building-management/energy-management systems.

Dokuz Eylül University publicly states that **17 buildings totaling about 236,000 m²** operate with integrated smart-building technologies and BMS monitoring/control for systems including lighting, heating, cooling, ventilation, water and safety.

Source:
https://greencampus.deu.edu.tr/green-campus/energy-and-climate-change/

Dumlupınar University's sustainability report describes a central Building Energy Management System (BEMS) used to monitor, record, analyze and control building energy consumption.

Source:
https://birimler.dpu.edu.tr/app/views/panel/ckfinder/userfiles/191/files/DPU__Sustainability_Report_2024-2025.pdf

Local vendors such as MetaBMS market AI-supported building and energy-management optimization in Türkiye:

https://metabms.com.tr/

## Implication

A future BOUNCAMPUS energy module should **integrate with or sit above existing BMS/BEMS**, not present basic telemetry/control as novel.

Possible higher-layer questions:

- Which anomalies deserve human attention first?
- Which intervention should be prioritized under a budget constraint?
- How should weather/occupancy/use be normalized before judging performance?
- Did an intervention actually save energy after adjustment?
- Can evidence flow from operational action into institutional sustainability reporting?

These are hypotheses, not known buyer pains.

# C-05 — In-house dashboards and reporting portals are a major substitute

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

Universities can build their own sustainability/GreenMetric dashboards. Universitas Multimedia Nusantara, for example, exposes a public GreenMetric dashboard with indicator trends and sustainability data.

Source:
https://greenmetric.umn.ac.id/

The practical substitute set therefore includes:

- Excel/Google Sheets;
- BI dashboards;
- internal data warehouses;
- BMS dashboards;
- university-built GreenMetric portals;
- sustainability-office manual evidence workflows;
- consultants and annual reporting projects.

**Research inference:** the largest competitor may be an existing internal workflow that is "good enough," not another startup.

# Competitive moat implications

## Not defensible alone

- "We centralize sustainability data."
- "We track carbon, energy, water and waste."
- "We support GreenMetric."
- "We have evidence/provenance."
- "We use AI."
- "We show a campus dashboard."

These capabilities already exist in the market in different combinations.

## More defensible if validated

A workflow-specific loop with accumulated intervention history:

```text
context + source data
       -> decision model
       -> operator recommendation
       -> override/action record
       -> measured outcome
       -> intervention-specific learning
```

Over time the valuable asset is not generic campus data; it is the institution-specific history of **which action worked under which operational context and constraints**.

# PMR competitive questions

Do not ask "Would you buy BOUNCAMPUS instead of X?"

Ask:

1. What software/spreadsheets are used today for food, energy, water, waste and sustainability reporting?
2. Which system is the source of truth for each metric?
3. Which tool is opened when an operational decision must be made — not when a report is prepared?
4. Does the institution use a BMS/BEMS, sustainability platform or food-waste system today?
5. What still requires Excel/email/phone calls between units?
6. What was the last decision that existing dashboards could not answer?
7. Can current tools record a recommendation, human override and later verified outcome?
8. If a GreenMetric/THE reporting platform already exists, what operational problem remains unsolved?
9. If food-waste measurement already exists, does it change production before the next service or only explain past waste?
10. What would make the institution replace a spreadsheet rather than keep it?
11. Which department owns the software budget and which department experiences the pain?
12. Is integration/procurement/security effort larger than the expected benefit?

---

# Product decision rule

After PMR, classify the opportunity:

### KEEP
Operators have a repeated, costly decision not solved by current tools; data is accessible enough; recommendations can change action before waste occurs.

### MODIFY
The pain exists but sits in measurement, contract/procurement coordination, reporting or another control point rather than production forecasting.

### KILL / pivot
Current systems already solve the decision well, the owner cannot act, or incremental benefit is too small relative to integration/procurement friction.

# Claim firewall

Do not claim:

- competitor feature absence without direct verification;
- vendor-reported reduction percentages as neutral evidence;
- a vendor is deployed at Boğaziçi without evidence;
- Emissary's İYTE public portal proves a paid commercial contract or full internal usage scope;
- BMS data will be accessible to BOUNCAMPUS;
- BOUNCAMPUS is unique because it has reporting/provenance/AI alone.
