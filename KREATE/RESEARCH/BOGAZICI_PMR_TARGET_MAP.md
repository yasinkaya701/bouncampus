# Boğaziçi Food-Waste PMR Target Map

**Research date:** 2026-10-01  
**Purpose:** Route interviews to the roles that can actually answer quantity, contract, control/acceptance, data, reservation, measurement and sustainability questions uncovered by the research packs.  
**Status:** Public professional-role mapping only. **No interview has occurred. No listed person implies endorsement, availability or validation.**

---

# 0. Use this map correctly

This is not a mass-outreach list.

Use the smallest number of interviews that resolve the highest-risk unknowns. Start with role-owned questions and ask for the next responsible person when the first interview reveals that the decision sits elsewhere.

Do not:

- treat public contact information as permission for repeated outreach;
- contact multiple people with the same generic message simultaneously;
- describe the product as deployed, validated or institutionally approved;
- ask IT for personal diner data;
- ask sustainability staff to validate a food-production workflow they do not own;
- treat a contractor corporate mailbox as proof that local Boğaziçi operations will respond;
- infer that a control/inspection body chooses daily production quantities;
- treat a historic special-period reservation rule as current normal-term policy.

### P0 interview questions

4. Can it be revised by campus, meal, package channel or batch?

### Evidence output required

A completed interview should produce a workflow of the form:

```text
role
→ input data / reservation / current heuristic
→ quantity or service-mode decision
→ deadline/freeze point
→ approval rights
→ contractor execution
→ control/acceptance
→ produced/delivered/served measurement
→ waste measurement
→ excess consequence
→ shortage consequence
→ payment/settlement signal
→ monthly reporting path
```

If the branch says the decision sits with TEMAŞ or another body, that is a useful finding, not a failed interview.

# 2. P0 — Contractor operations: direct production user candidate

## TARGET-BU-02 — TEMAŞ Gıda / Boğaziçi project operations

**Why:** Public procurement research identifies TEMAŞ as the current 2026–2027 contractor. Sector precedents from Uşak and Kırıkkale show that in some university contracts the contractor is directly responsible for forecasting daily production while payment is tied to actual consumption.

### Public corporate contact surface

- Company: **TEMAŞ Gıda Sanayi ve Ticaret A.Ş.**
- Corporate collaboration/contact mailbox: `info@temasgida.com`
- Corporate phone: +90 212 886 41 41
- Public website: https://www.temasgida.com/
- Contact page: https://www.temasgida.com/iletisim

The public corporate page does **not** identify the current Boğaziçi project manager. Ask Food Services for the correct local operations contact rather than guessing a person.

### P0 interview questions

1. Who predicts tomorrow's quantity for the Boğaziçi project?
2. What heuristic is used today: same weekday, recent average, operator experience, reservation, university request, or another method?
3. If you receive a reservation count `R`, how do you transform it into production `Q`?
4. What buffer is added for no-shows, walk-ins, menu uncertainty or late demand?
5. What time do ingredient commitment and cooking become irreversible?
6. Is production split into batches that can react to early demand?
7. How is output allocated across six campuses and package/kiosk channels?
8. Can service mode switch between hall/package/delivery based on expected demand?
9. What does a 5% overforecast actually cost operationally?
10. What does a 5% underforecast cause — emergency cooking, service delay, penalty, complaint or other cost?
11. Which quantity is recorded for hakediş?
12. What fields are recorded internally: requested, produced, delivered, served, returned, leftover, discarded?
13. Which of those records are shared with the university/control organisation?
14. Would a range/shortage-risk recommendation be more usable than one scalar prediction?
15. What is the latest timestamp at which a new signal can still change quantity, batch, campus allocation or service mode?

### Critical commercial question

> If planning improves, **who captures the financial benefit — TEMAŞ, Boğaziçi, both, or neither under the contract?**

This determines whether the product is university SaaS, contractor operations software, shared decision support, or the wrong intervention altogether.

# 10. Recommended interview order

The fastest current sequence is:

```text
1. Food Services Branch manager
   ↓ identify actual quantity/service-mode owner + current reservation usage + settlement question
2. TEMAŞ local project/production operator
   ↓ identify heuristic, freeze time, buffer, risk, batch/allocation and internal records
3. Dining/Cooking/Distribution Control Organisation
   ↓ identify accepted truth, approval boundary, shortage/deviation and contract-control records
4. BİD / BUCard data owner
   ↓ test privacy-safe reservation + service event availability against the accepted operational record
5. Safe & Sustainable Food / monthly reporting owner
   ↓ reconcile produced-consumed-discarded definitions and report-to-decision flow
6. Waste/sustainability measurement specialist
   ↓ validate physical waste boundary and impact claim rules
7. SKS senior owner only for permissions/escalation
```

This order avoids asking IT for a dataset before knowing which event should be the ground truth.

# 12. Highest-value outcome

The single most useful PMR result is now a verified answer to:

> **Under the current Boğaziçi–TEMAŞ workflow, who chooses each meal's production quantity / allocation / service mode, at what exact time does each choice become costly to change, which reservation/access/operational signals exist before that moment, which record is accepted as truth afterward, and who bears the cost of excess versus shortage?**

Everything else — model choice, app UI, carbon conversion and market story — should remain downstream of that answer.
