# Business Model & Public Procurement Entry Research — 2026-10-04

**Purpose:** bound plausible pilot-to-purchase routes for public-university customers without inventing willingness to pay or procurement shortcuts.  
**Status:** secondary regulatory/process research. **Not legal advice, not proof Boğaziçi can or will purchase BOUNCAMPUS through any particular method.**

## Executive conclusion

A public-university software/decision-support sale is not simply:

```text
operator likes product -> subscription starts
```

The commercial path can involve:

- budget owner;
- procurement method;
- price/threshold;
- technical specification;
- IT/security/data approval;
- service/vendor contract boundaries;
- pilot permission;
- economic beneficiary who may differ from purchaser.

For BOUNCAMPUS, PMR must separate **pilot feasibility** from **paid procurement feasibility**.

# 1. Current 2026 public-procurement thresholds are material constraints, not pricing targets

Kamu İhale Kurumu's official 2026 comparative threshold table states that for the period **1 Feb 2026–31 Jan 2027**:

- 4734 / 22(d) direct-procurement monetary limit for administrations within metropolitan municipality boundaries: **1,021,827 TRY**;
- 4734 / 22(d) for other administrations: **340,391 TRY**;
- 4734 / 21(f) negotiated-procedure limit for goods/material/service purchases: **3,406,508 TRY**.

Official source:
https://dosyalar.kik.gov.tr/yardim/dokumanlar/2026_Esik_Degerler_Parasal_Limitler_Karsilastirma.pdf

KİK announcement confirming the updated values take effect on 1 Feb 2026:
https://www.ihale.gov.tr/Duyuru/795/esik_degerler_ve_parasal_limitler_ilan_ucretleri_ve_e-ihale_hizmet_bedelinin_1_subat_2026_tarihi_itibari_ile_guncellenmesi_nedeni_ile_ekap_uzerinde_yurutulecek_islemlerde_dikkat_edilecek_hususlar.html

### Important boundary

These are **legal/procedural limits**, not:

- BOUNCAMPUS pricing guidance;
- proof a software/service purchase qualifies under 22(d) or 21(f);
- proof Boğaziçi has an available budget;
- proof the buyer would prefer those methods;
- proof splitting a larger need into smaller purchases is permissible.

Do not reverse-engineer product price solely to sit under a procurement threshold.

# 2. Annual limit control also matters

KİK training material states that expenditures under the monetary limits of Articles 21 and 22 are subject to a rule under 4734/62(ı): their annual total cannot exceed **10% of the corresponding appropriations** without the Public Procurement Board's favorable opinion.

Official KİK training source:
https://ekapakademi.kik.gov.tr/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/4734-Ortak-Hukumler-V1.14.pdf

### GTM implication

Even a purchase below the headline 22(d) threshold is not automatically frictionless.

PMR / buyer discovery must ask:

- what budget line would fund the tool;
- who controls it;
- what procurement method is normally used for operational software/services;
- whether a research/pilot phase can occur before procurement;
- what documentation/security review is required.

KİK announced in July 2026 a renewed direct-procurement application and publication page, including electronic price-offer workflows through EKAP.

### Implication

Useful whole-product artifacts may eventually include:

- data-processing boundary;
- service-level/support terms;
- pilot measurement protocol;
- price breakdown;
- technical acceptance criteria.

These are not needed for the Oct 8 application but matter for a credible GTM roadmap.

# 4. Buyer route depends on the contract economics

Current research has at least three commercial structures:

## U1 — university captures direct economic value

Possible when university payment/cost changes with avoidable quantity.

Candidate buyer route:

```text
Food Services/SKS champion
-> IT/data/security
-> budget/procurement owner
```

## C1 — contractor captures direct economic value

Possible when contractor bears ingredient/excess cost and is paid on served/accepted quantity.

Candidate route:

```text
local project champion
-> regional/central operations
-> contractor IT/finance/procurement
```

## J1 — shared operational value

Possible when authority, cost and service-risk are split between university and contractor.

Candidate product form:

> shared decision/evidence layer with both parties seeing recommendation, override and outcome history.

### Critical unknown

Boğaziçi/TEMAŞ current settlement semantics are not publicly resolved.

Do not choose the economic buyer until PMR/contract evidence clarifies them.

# 5. Pilot route and production purchase are separate decisions

A bounded PMR/measurement or research pilot may have a different approval route from a paid production deployment.

Keep two maps:

## Pilot approval map

```text
operational permission
+ contractor permission where needed
+ data/privacy/security approval
+ food-safety / physical-install approval if hardware used
```

## Paid deployment map

```text
validated user need
+ economic buyer
+ budget
+ procurement route
+ technical/security acceptance
+ contract/support
```

A free or research pilot does **not** prove willingness to pay.

A paid purchase discussion does **not** prove users will adopt the workflow.

# 6. Business-model hypotheses to test

Do not submit these as facts.

## BM-A — university SaaS/service

Annual institutional license + onboarding/integration.

Needs:
- university owns value;
- procurement feasible;
- workflow broad enough to justify recurring fee.

## BM-B — contractor enterprise license

Central catering operator licenses the decision layer across multiple projects.

Needs:
- company owns forecast-error economics;
- tooling selected centrally;
- workflows/data can be standardized across projects.

## BM-C — site/project subscription

One contract/site pays per kitchen or campus.

Needs:
- project P&L can authorize value;
- central rollout unnecessary;
- support cost remains reasonable.

## BM-D — measurement + decision package

Software plus optional TrayGate/measurement equipment where data are missing.

Needs:
- sensing gap materially blocks value;
- hardware economics and maintenance work;
- installed measurement beats manual/available alternatives.

## BM-E — pilot / implementation service then recurring software

Initial paid or sponsored measurement/integration engagement followed by recurring decision software.

Needs:
- pilot has standalone buyer value;
- conversion path exists;
- implementation burden does not dominate lifetime value.

No model should be selected before buyer PMR.

# 7. Pricing research must come after QVP

Do not start with:

```text
what can we charge below the threshold?
```

Start with:

```text
what measurable priority changes?
who captures that value?
how much is it worth operationally/economically?
what is the credible alternative cost?
```

Then test pricing/payment structure with the economic buyer.

Potential value bases — to be validated:

- annual/site license;
- kitchen/site tier;
- meal-volume tier;
- enterprise contractor license;
- fixed pilot fee;
- hardware + software/support.

Avoid savings-share pricing until savings can be measured, attributed and financially reconciled.

---

# 8. Questions for a public-university economic buyer / procurement owner

- Which unit would procure a tool that changes dining production planning?
- Would it be treated as software, consulting/service, data/analytics service or another category?
- What approvals are required before a pilot?
- What changes for a paid production deployment?
- Can the relevant unit fund a bounded pilot independently?
- Which IT/security/privacy documents are mandatory?
- Would the existing catering contract need amendment/approval?
- Is procurement annual, multi-year or project-specific?
- What purchasing route has been used for comparable operational software?

These questions establish process; they should not lead with legal terminology unless the stakeholder uses it.

---

# 9. Application / pitch language

Safe:

> Our initial buyer route is still a hypothesis because the university, contractor and economic beneficiary can differ by contract. PMR will identify who owns the decision, who captures value and who can authorize a pilot.

Unsafe:

> Universities can buy us directly because we are below the direct-procurement threshold.

Unsafe:

> The contractor will pay because it saves food cost.

Neither is currently supported for Boğaziçi.

---

## Current conclusion

The commercial problem is not simply price.

The key unknown is the alignment of:

```text
user
+ decision authority
+ economic beneficiary
+ buyer
+ procurement route
```

A strong BOUNCAMPUS beachhead should minimize fragmentation across these roles or provide enough value to coordinate them.
