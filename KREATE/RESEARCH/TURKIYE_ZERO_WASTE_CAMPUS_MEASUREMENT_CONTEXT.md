# Türkiye Zero-Waste & Campus Food-Waste Measurement Context

**Research date:** 2026-10-01  
**Purpose:** Ground KREATE pilot measurement and climate language in official Türkiye Zero Waste guidance plus current UNEP food-waste framing, without converting global statistics into unsupported Boğaziçi impact claims.  
**Status:** Secondary/public-source research only. **NOT legal advice. NOT a compliance certification. NOT measured Boğaziçi impact.**

---

# 0. Executive conclusion

The strongest policy/climate framing for the current product is not `AI cuts carbon`.

It is:

> Türkiye's Zero Waste framework places prevention/reduction, monitoring and record-keeping inside the waste-management system; university-specific guidance explicitly asks institutions to organize, plan, monitor, keep records and revise. UNEP similarly emphasizes measurement as the basis for food-waste reduction. Therefore a credible KREATE pilot should produce decision-linked physical evidence first: produced/served/excess quantities, consistently measured food-waste mass and shortage/service guardrails.

Only after measured waste reduction exists should the team translate it into climate/resource equivalents using a documented conversion method.

# 1. Türkiye Zero Waste framework — prevention comes before downstream handling

## ZW-01 — Official regulation defines zero waste around prevention and reduction

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

The published Zero Waste Regulation describes the system as covering the chain from **prevention of waste generation**, through reduction, source separation, temporary storage, separate collection, transport and processing.

The regulation definitions also describe:

- `atık azaltımı` as reducing waste quantity through planned preventive measures in production, consumption and service processes;
- `önleme` as measures taken before a material becomes waste;
- `biyo-bozunur atık` as including food and kitchen waste arising from homes, offices, restaurants, sales points, canteens, food preparation and food-processing facilities.

Official Ministry page reproducing the regulation:  
https://erzincan.csb.gov.tr/sifir-atik-yonetmeligi-resmi-gazetede-yayinlandi-haber-240271

### KREATE implication

A production-decision intervention sits **upstream** of composting, recycling or disposal. If it truly avoids unnecessary production, it belongs conceptually to prevention/reduction rather than merely better waste routing.

Do not claim that the product itself fulfills regulatory compliance; that requires a separate legal/compliance assessment.

# 2. University-specific official guidance emphasizes monitoring, records and revision

## ZW-02 — Universities are told to monitor, keep records and revise

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

The official Zero Waste university guidance describes a workflow including:

1. create the working team;
2. plan the system;
3. train and implement;
4. separate and route wastes;
5. **monitor, keep records and revise**;
6. record outputs such as quantities sent to facilities and resulting gains.

Official university guidance:  
https://sifiratik.gov.tr/SistemKurulumu/universiteler-icin

The general system setup page also says institutions should first assess their current situation and plan institution-specific actions; needs are identified by unit, explicitly including places such as the **cafeteria/yemekhane**.

Official setup page:  
https://www.sifiratik.gov.tr/SistemKurulumu

### KREATE implication

The pilot should create a traceable measurement artifact rather than only a dashboard screenshot:

```text
baseline period
measurement boundary
intervention decision
physical quantities
service guardrails
source/provenance
review notes
```

This aligns with the existing institutional language of monitoring and records while remaining a separate pilot artifact.

# 3. Official public-sector implementation shows a practical cafeteria measurement pattern

## ZW-03 — Ministry cafeteria example measures waste by type and mass

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

The Ministry's own Zero Waste implementation page describes a cafeteria where waste categories are separately collected; incoming waste at the temporary storage area is **weighed and recorded in kilograms**. It separately discusses food/bread waste, organic waste and used cooking oil.

Official source:  
https://destek.csb.gov.tr/bakanligimizda-sifir-atik-uygulamalari-99118

### Pilot implication

A practical food-service pilot should distinguish at minimum:

```text
unserved_edible_surplus
preparation_or_kitchen_waste
plate_waste
used_oil_or_other_separate_streams  # only if in scope
```

If the site cannot separate those categories reliably, record the limitation explicitly. A single mixed `waste_kg` column may be insufficient to attribute change to production forecasting.

# 4. National Zero Waste reporting is built around inventory and monitoring

## ZW-04 — The national system is used for inventory, monitoring and reporting

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

The Ministry's environmental indicators page states that the Zero Waste Information System was created for data entry, inventory creation, monitoring activities, reporting and the certification process. Users include public institutions and educational institutions.

Official source:  
https://cevreselgostergeler.csb.gov.tr/sifir-atik-yonetim-sistemi-108178

A March 2026 Ministry update reports that by the end of 2025 the Zero Waste Management System had been established in **217,000 buildings/sites**, with Türkiye's reported recovery rate at **37.53% in 2025**.

Official source:  
https://cygm.csb.gov.tr/haberler/sifir-atik-ta-geri-kazanim-orani-37-53-e-ulasti-304527

### Claim boundary

These national figures demonstrate policy scale and monitoring infrastructure. They do **not** prove a market for this product, Boğaziçi's compliance state, or a causal relationship between this product and national recovery targets.

# 5. Global food-waste context — use only as context, not local impact

## CLIMATE-01 — Food service is a material global food-waste sector

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

UNEP's current Zero Waste / Food Waste framing reports that in 2022 about **1.05 billion tonnes** of food were wasted at household, food-service and retail levels. The 2026 International Day of Zero Waste material attributes the global split approximately as:

- households: 60%;
- **food service: 28%**;
- retail: 12%.

Official UNEP source:  
https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/press-release/food-waste-focus-world-marks-international-day-zero-waste

### Claim boundary

This supports food service as a globally material category. It does not establish Boğaziçi's causal drivers or how much of its 48,251 kg public total is preventable through production forecasting.

---

## CLIMATE-02 — Food loss and waste are associated with 8–10% of global GHG emissions

`PUBLIC SOURCE`

UNEP states that food loss and waste together account for roughly **8–10% of global greenhouse-gas emissions**. UNEP also notes that wasted food embodies land, water, energy and other resources used across production and supply chains.

Official UNEP sources:  
https://www.unep.org/topics/food-systems/food-loss-and-waste/delivering-sdg-123  
https://www.unep.org/interactives/beat-waste-pollution/index.html

### Critical rule

**Never multiply Boğaziçi food-waste kilograms by a generic global emissions factor and label the result measured CO₂ savings.**

Food composition, production method, transport, cooking, waste treatment and system boundaries matter. A climate conversion must state:

```text
measured avoided waste by food category
conversion dataset / emission factor
system boundary
assumptions
uncertainty
whether avoided production is actually demonstrated
```

Until then, carbon impact remains `MODEL ESTIMATE`, not pilot truth.

# 6. SDG 12.3 provides a measurement-and-reduction frame

## CLIMATE-03 — SDG 12.3 targets food-waste reduction by 2030

UNEP, custodian of the food-waste indicator, describes SDG 12.3 as aiming to halve global per-capita food waste at retail and consumer levels by 2030 and reduce food loss across supply chains. UNEP emphasizes measuring food waste to establish the problem scale, identify opportunities and track progress.

Official UNEP pages:  
https://www.unep.org/topics/food-systems/food-loss-and-waste/delivering-sdg-123  
https://www.unep.org/resources/publication/food-waste-index-report-2024

### Application implication

Use SDG 12.3 as a **directional policy context**, not as evidence that a specific Boğaziçi reduction percentage is achievable.

# 7. Recommended KREATE pilot KPI hierarchy

The research supports the following order.

## Tier 1 — Primary physical decision metrics

```text
produced_portions
served_portions
unserved_edible_surplus
shortage_or_sellout_events
emergency_top_up
operator_override
```

These directly test whether the recommendation improves the production decision.

## Tier 2 — Waste measurement

Prefer:

```text
unserved_edible_surplus_kg
preparation_waste_kg
plate_waste_kg
```

Normalize when useful by:

```text
kg_waste_per_100_served_meals
```

The denominator and boundary must remain identical across control/intervention periods.

## Tier 3 — Economic metrics

Only after contract semantics are verified:

```text
variable_food_cost_exposure
contract_accepted_quantity
emergency_shortage_cost
labor_or_logistics_delta
```

Do not infer these from total contract value.

## Tier 4 — Climate/resource model estimates

Only after measured physical reduction:

```text
estimated_CO2e_avoided
estimated_water_embodied_avoided
estimated_land_or_resource_effect
```

Every value must be labeled `MODEL ESTIMATE` and carry method, source and uncertainty.

# 8. Measurement boundary proposed for the pilot

## Before production

```text
forecast_timestamp
quantity_freeze_timestamp
planned_quantity
recommended_quantity
operator_final_quantity
reason_codes
```

## During/after service

```text
produced_quantity
served_or_validated_actual_demand
shortage_event
emergency_top_up
unserved_edible_surplus
```

## Waste audit

```text
unserved_edible_surplus_kg
preparation_waste_kg
plate_waste_kg
measurement_method
measurement_timestamp
scale_or_source
reviewer
```

## Destination, when relevant

```text
donation_or_safe_redistribution
animal_shelter_or_feed_route
compost
licensed_recovery
municipal_or_other_disposal
```

Destination is useful for waste-management accounting, but **prevention should not be conflated with diversion**. Moving the same excess food to compost is not the same intervention as avoiding unnecessary production.

# 9. Anti-greenwashing rules for all agents

1. `48,251 kg reported food waste` is a public scale fact, not forecast-caused waste.
2. `waste diverted from disposal` is not automatically `waste prevented`.
3. `food saved` requires a consistent before/after measurement boundary.
4. `CO2e saved` requires measured physical change plus documented conversion; otherwise label `MODEL ESTIMATE`.
5. `water saved` must not be inferred from generic water-footprint averages without food composition and method.
6. A global 8–10% GHG statistic cannot be converted into a local percentage impact.
7. Contract cost, food procurement value and avoidable waste cost are different quantities.
8. A successful prediction is not environmental impact unless operators act on it and measured waste changes without unacceptable shortage.

# 10. PMR questions for Zero Waste / sustainability owners

1. Which food-waste categories are currently weighed separately?
2. What is the exact boundary of the 48,251 kg public figure?
3. Is the measurement by generation date or collection date?
4. Can data be separated by campus, meal and pre-/post-consumer waste?
5. Which existing Zero Waste records are considered authoritative internally?
6. Who reviews monthly food-waste numbers before public reporting?
7. Which metric would count as a meaningful reduction without compromising food service?
8. Does the sustainability/Zero Waste team influence production quantity, or only downstream waste handling?
9. What evidence would they require before allowing a climate-impact claim?
10. Which conversion methodology, if any, does the university already use for carbon/water claims from food waste?

# 12. Bottom line

The climate narrative should follow this chain:

```text
real production decision
→ measured physical excess / waste
→ verified reduction without shortage
→ documented contract/economic effect
→ optional transparent climate/resource conversion
```

not:

```text
AI prediction
→ generic food-waste statistic
→ claimed CO2 savings
```

This makes the project more credible to KREATE reviewers and gives EE/CS1 a concrete measurement contract while protecting CS2 from unsupported impact claims.
