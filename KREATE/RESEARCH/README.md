# KREATE Research Packs

This directory contains secondary/public-source research and research-derived protocols prepared for KREATE agents. It is a shared **research layer**, not the evidence registry and not PMR.

## Claim boundary

- Nothing in this directory counts as `INTERVIEW EVIDENCE` or customer validation.
- Official university pages and reports may become `PUBLIC_SOURCE` evidence only after a domain owner narrows the claim and promotes it into `../EVIDENCE.md`.
- Academic papers are benchmarks and design references. A result from another university does **not** prove the same mechanism exists at Boğaziçi.
- Pilot protocols in this directory are **proposed methods, not completed experiments or measured impact**.
- Apparent inconsistencies in source data are preserved as caveats; agents must not silently repair them.
- Model or scenario implications derived from these sources remain `HYPOTHESIS`, `POLICY_HEURISTIC`, or `MODEL ESTIMATE` until separately validated.
- Procurement samples in this directory are **not** TAM/SAM/SOM unless a separate systematic market-sizing method explicitly establishes that scope.
- Contract clauses from another university are **sector precedents, not Boğaziçi contract facts**.
- Global climate/food-waste statistics are context, **not local conversion factors or measured Boğaziçi impact**.
- Public professional contact information is for role routing only; it is **not interview evidence, endorsement, availability or permission for repeated outreach**.
- Policy/ranking pressure is market context, **not willingness-to-pay evidence or a guaranteed score/compliance outcome**.
- Vendor claims and case-study percentages are competitive context, **not neutral evidence or expected BOUNCAMPUS impact**.
- National university counts are ecosystem context, **not addressable-customer counts or TAM without segmentation evidence**.
- Reservation practices at other universities are sector precedents, **not evidence that Boğaziçi should use the same mechanism in normal-term service**.
- Historical Boğaziçi reservation thresholds and special-period rules are **regime-specific evidence, not current universal operating policy**.
- User meal charging and contractor settlement are separate economic layers until contract/hakediş semantics are verified.
- Formal governance responsibility does not establish the daily operational decision owner.

## How agents should consume a pack

1. Read the **agent-critical takeaways** and **claim firewall** first.
2. Prefer IDs from machine-readable companions when creating downstream tasks.
3. Before promoting a public number into `../EVIDENCE.md`, reopen its source, verify wording/date/units and narrow the claim.
4. Route unknown workflow facts to PMR rather than assumptions.
5. Route quantitative/model implications to reproducible technical tests rather than presenting them as measured campus performance.
6. Preserve `source`, `year`, `version`, `unit`, `period`, `scope`, `service_regime`, `event_stage` and `counting_semantics`; use `UNKNOWN` instead of inference.
7. Never translate procurement contract value into food-waste savings unless payable/accepted quantity semantics are verified.
8. Never extrapolate a purposive procurement sample into national TAM without methodology and denominator.
9. Never copy another university's contract clause into a Boğaziçi claim.
10. Never convert global food-waste factors into local saved CO2e/water without measured physical change and documented conversion.
11. A contact or scheduled meeting is not PMR evidence; only completed interview artifacts enter the interview evidence pipeline.
12. Treat policy/rankings as context pressure, not willingness-to-pay evidence.
13. Treat external academic intervention effects as design references, not expected Boğaziçi effects.
14. Preserve event-time, reporting-period, source-version and measurement-stage semantics; represent conflicts explicitly.
15. Verify competitor capabilities before claiming a gap; vendor claims are not neutral evidence.
16. Do not turn a national institution count into an addressable market count before workflow/buying-path evidence.
17. Do not defend ML if reservation/intent or a simple rule removes uncertainty more cheaply and reliably.
18. Never equate reservation, BUCard charge, turnstile event, meal served, accepted service and contractor-settled meal without owner reconciliation.
19. Never promote a historical special-period threshold to current policy without effective-date/regime validation.
20. Formal governance responsibility does not equal daily operational authority; verify decision rights with a real workflow.
21. A proposed pilot protocol does not become `TECH_TEST` evidence until run outputs, method/version and limitations are recorded.

## Current cross-pack synthesis

```text
PUBLIC FACT:
Boğaziçi already has sustainability measurement/governance,
digital meal-access infrastructure, special-period reservation capability,
service-regime switching, formal food-service control and a measured waste stream.

POLICY FACT:
Current food governance explicitly includes production planning and
monthly recording/reporting of produced, consumed and discarded food quantities/types.

MARKET FACT:
Türkiye already has sustainability platforms, food-waste specialists,
BMS/BEMS, in-house tools and reservation-based demand signals.

RESEARCH INFERENCE:
A first-time dashboard/reporting pitch is weak;
provenance alone is not a moat;
ML is not automatically the best control mechanism.

BOĞAZİÇİ-SPECIFIC CONTROL EVIDENCE:
Reservation count historically changed service modality in a special regime,
and a 2026 holiday flow attached a consequence to uncancelled no-shows.

UNKNOWN:
Who owns the normal-term quantity/allocation/service-mode decision,
when it freezes, what residual uncertainty remains,
which record is accepted as truth, and who captures the economic benefit.

PMR JOB:
Resolve owner + signal + freeze + action + approval + accepted truth + incentives.

TECH JOB:
Reconcile reservation → service first.
Compare raw reservation → simple correction → passive history.
Use contextual residual ML only if material predictable error remains.

PILOT PATH:
retrospective reconciliation
→ transparent baseline
→ residual model only if justified
→ shadow decision
→ advisory human-in-loop
→ bounded prospective intervention.

PLATFORM DIRECTION IF VALIDATED:
Observe -> Reconcile -> Infer Intent/Forecast -> Diagnose
-> Recommend Action/Regime -> Human Act -> Verify.
```
