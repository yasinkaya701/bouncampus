# Climate & Policy Importance — 2026-10-04

**Purpose:** support the `why is this important and worth solving?` narrative without inventing Boğaziçi-specific climate savings.  
**Status:** public/global policy context. **Not measured BOUNCAMPUS impact.**

## Executive conclusion

Food waste is a material global climate/resource problem, and **prevention at source is environmentally preferable to managing waste after it is created**.

Türkiye's Zero Waste framework also explicitly begins with prevention/reduction and treats food/kitchen waste as biodegradable waste within a traceable management system.

That supports BOUNCAMPUS's prevention-first logic if PMR confirms that production surplus is a real controllable waste stream.

It does **not** support a Boğaziçi-specific CO2e, water or financial-savings number before a measured pilot and context-appropriate conversion factors exist, and it does not mean BOUNCAMPUS itself is a legal requirement.

# 1. Global food-waste scale

UNEP's `Food Waste Index Report 2024` key messages state that in **2022**:

- the world wasted approximately **1.05 billion tonnes of food** at household, food-service and retail levels;
- this represented roughly **19% of food available to consumers**;
- households accounted for about 631 million tonnes;
- food service about **290 million tonnes**;
- retail the remainder.

Source:
https://wedocs.unep.org/bitstream/handle/20.500.11822/45275/Food-Waste-Index-2024-key-messages.pdf

### Safe application use

> Food service is one of the major consumer-level food-waste sectors globally.

### Unsafe jump

> Therefore Boğaziçi's waste is primarily caused by overproduction.

UNEP establishes scale, not local cause.

# 2. Why prevention is different from recovery

EPA's current Wasted Food Scale places **prevent wasted food** at the top/preferred tier and states that prevention avoids the upstream resources and environmental impacts associated with producing, processing, distributing and preparing food that is never eaten.

Sources:

- https://www.epa.gov/sustainable-management-food/wasted-food-scale
- https://www.epa.gov/sustainable-management-food/prevent-wasted-food-through-source-reduction

EPA's `From Field to Bin` synthesis states that source reduction has environmental benefits substantially greater than most downstream food-waste management pathways because it avoids the need to produce the wasted food in the first place.

Source:
https://www.epa.gov/land-research/field-bin-environmental-impacts-us-food-waste-management-pathways

### Product implication

If BOUNCAMPUS prevents unnecessary production, it targets a different environmental stage from:

- composting;
- anaerobic digestion;
- landfill diversion;
- waste collection reporting.

Recovery/diversion remains useful, but it is not equivalent to prevention.

# 3. Prevention still requires diagnosis

EPA guidance for businesses/food-service operations recommends assessing:

- amount of wasted food;
- type;
- reason;

before choosing reduction strategies.

It lists source-reduction actions such as:

- matching purchasing/inventory with ordering;
- modifying menus;
- improving production/handling;
- storage improvements;
- reducing serving sizes where appropriate.

Sources:

- https://www.epa.gov/sustainable-management-food/prevent-wasted-food-through-source-reduction
- https://www.epa.gov/sustainable-management-food/tools-preventing-and-diverting-wasted-food

### Strong alignment with BOUNCAMPUS research

The product should not assume `forecasting` before diagnosing the reason/stage.

The operating logic should be:

```text
measure amount/type/reason
-> identify controllable decision
-> intervene upstream where possible
-> verify outcome
```

# 4. Why downstream recovery alone is not enough for the product thesis

Boğaziçi publicly reports recovery/composting activity alongside total food waste.

That is positive sustainability infrastructure, but BOUNCAMPUS should not present recovery as proof that the upstream problem is solved.

A prevention product asks:

> Can avoidable food be prevented from becoming waste in the first place?

This is complementary to recovery systems rather than a replacement.

### Boundary

Do not say:

> `Recovered food waste has no environmental value.`

That is false/overbroad.

Safe:

> prevention is generally prioritized above downstream management when avoidable waste can be prevented safely.

# 5. Global emissions context — use carefully

EPA's sustainable-management guidance cites the widely used global estimate that food loss/waste is associated with roughly **8–10% of global greenhouse-gas emissions**.

Source:
https://www.epa.gov/sustainable-management-food/sustainable-management-food-basics

### Application use

This can support why food waste matters globally.

### Do not calculate

```text
48,251 kg Boğaziçi waste × generic kgCO2e/kg = achieved BOUNCAMPUS impact
```

Food categories, sourcing, preparation and waste stage vary substantially.

A future climate conversion needs:

```text
measured food avoided
× food/category/context-specific factor
+ documented scope/uncertainty
```

# 7. Türkiye Zero Waste framework explicitly includes prevention and food/kitchen waste

The official Zero Waste Regulation defines the zero-waste management system as beginning with:

- prevention of waste generation;
- reduction of waste;
- source separation;
- temporary storage;
- collection and processing.

It also defines `biyo-bozunur atık` to include food and kitchen waste originating from settings such as offices, restaurants, sales points, canteens, food preparation and food-processing facilities.

Official ministry/regulation source:
https://erzincan.csb.gov.tr/sifir-atik-yonetmeligi-resmi-gazetede-yayinlandi-haber-240271

The Ministry's current regulation index continues to list the Zero Waste Regulation as the governing framework:
https://cygm.csb.gov.tr/yonetmelikler-440

Official Zero Waste implementation guidance separately identifies waste streams generated by food-preparation and dining/restaurant areas, including organic waste.

Guidance source:
https://webdosya.csb.gov.tr/db/sifiratik/menu/sifir-atik-sistemi_20180604124110.pdf

### Strategic implication

The prevention-first product narrative is aligned with the logic of Türkiye's institutional waste-management framework:

```text
prevent / reduce first
then separate / collect / recover what still arises
```

### Strong boundary

Do **not** say:

> BOUNCAMPUS is legally required by the Zero Waste Regulation.

Do **not** say:

> universities must buy an AI food-waste prevention system.

The regulation creates institutional waste-management context; it does not prescribe this product or prove purchase urgency.

# 8. Reporting/traceability is relevant but not sufficient value

The Zero Waste framework includes registration, monitoring, certification and traceability concepts.

This makes evidence/provenance useful in a whole product, but `we provide reporting` is weak differentiation because:

- universities already operate Zero Waste processes;
- specialist sustainability platforms already support reporting;
- the KREATE thesis is stronger when evidence is tied to a **real operational decision before waste is created**.

The product opportunity to test is therefore:

```text
reported waste / existing operational signal
        ↓
root-cause diagnosis
        ↓
preventive decision
        ↓
measured result
        ↓
traceable evidence
```

not merely another annual waste report.

# 10. Problem-importance narrative ladder

Use four layers rather than one inflated claim.

## Layer A — global

Food waste is globally material and food service contributes a major share of consumer-level waste.

## Layer B — policy / institutional

Türkiye's Zero Waste framework prioritizes prevention/reduction before downstream management, while higher education has active sustainability/digital-governance agendas.

## Layer C — local

Boğaziçi publicly reports a measurable food-waste stream and operates high-volume multi-campus dining.

## Layer D — intervention hypothesis

The team is testing whether a material share of avoidable waste can be prevented through an upstream operational decision such as production quantity/batch/allocation.

This structure avoids turning global/policy evidence into unsupported local causality.

# 12. Strategic conclusion

The climate/policy case becomes strongest when the product does **not** begin with carbon accounting or compliance software.

It begins with a physical operational question:

```text
Was avoidable food prevented from being unnecessarily prepared/discarded
without worsening meal availability or food-service constraints?
```

Only after that physical effect is measured should BOUNCAMPUS derive climate/resource impact and feed traceable evidence into institutional sustainability processes.
